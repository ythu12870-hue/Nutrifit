Obese

Obese Recommendation

Goal: Safely improve fitness, mobility, strength, and sustainable health habits.

Exercise

Low-Impact Activity

Start with 10–20 mins of easy walking

Gentle Strength

2–3 days/wk: Chair squats, wall push-ups, resistance bands

Safety

Progress gradually; stop if pain or dizziness occurs

Nutrition

Diet Pattern

Regular meals with protein, veggies, whole grains, and water

To Avoid

Sugary drinks, processed foods, skipping meals, or extreme fasting

Key Focus

Focus on sustainable habits and consult a professional for personalized guidance.

Important: BMI is a screening measure, not a complete assessment of health. For children and teenagers, BMI is normally interpreted using age- and sex-specific growth charts. Use this page as general wellness information and ask a qualified health professional for personalized advice.
