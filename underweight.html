Underweight

Underweight Recommendation

Goal: Build strength, fitness, and overall health.

Exercise

Strength Training

3 days/wk: 1–2 sets × 8–15 reps (Squats, push-ups, rows, lunges, glute bridges)

Light Cardio

2–3 days/wk: Walking, cycling, or swimming

Recovery

Rest between sessions

Nutrition

Recommended Foods

Whole grains, lean meats, eggs, dairy, nuts/peanut butter, avocado, fruits, and vegetables

Meal Pattern

3 main meals + 2–3 snacks daily

Key Focus

Focus on eating enough nutritious food and getting sufficient sleep rather than calorie counting

Important: BMI is a screening measure, not a complete assessment of health. For children and teenagers, BMI is normally interpreted using age- and sex-specific growth charts. Use this page as general wellness information and ask a qualified health professional for personalized advice.
