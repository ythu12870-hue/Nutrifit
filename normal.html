Normal Weight

Normal Weight Recommendation

Goal: Maintain a healthy weight, overall fitness, and strength.

Exercise

Strength Training

3 days/wk: Target all major muscle groups (legs, chest, back, shoulders, core)

Cardio/Sports

2–3 days/wk: 20–40 minutes of cardio or sports

Daily Activity

Light movement or walking

Nutrition

Plate Method

½ vegetables & fruits, ¼ whole grains (rice, oats), ¼ protein (chicken, fish, eggs, beans) + dairy as suitable

Meal Pattern

Regular balanced meals

Key Focus

Eat according to individual energy needs rather than using a fixed calorie target.

Important: BMI is a screening measure, not a complete assessment of health. For children and teenagers, BMI is normally interpreted using age- and sex-specific growth charts. Use this page as general wellness information and ask a qualified health professional for personalized advice.
