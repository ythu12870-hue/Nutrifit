Overweight

Overweight Recommendation

Goal: Safely improve cardiovascular fitness, strength, mobility, and overall health.

Exercise

Strength Training

3 days/wk: Beginner, joint-friendly exercises

Low-Impact Cardio

3–5 days/wk: Walking, cycling, or swimming (20–30 mins, gradually increasing)

Pacing

Choose joint-comfortable movements and build duration over time

Nutrition

Recommended Foods

Whole grains, chicken, fish, eggs, beans, vegetables, fruits, and water

To Reduce

Sugary drinks, processed snacks, and large portions

Key Focus

Focus on sustainable habits rather than strict calorie cutting unless medically advised.

Important: BMI is a screening measure, not a complete assessment of health. For children and teenagers, BMI is normally interpreted using age- and sex-specific growth charts. Use this page as general wellness information and ask a qualified health professional for personalized advice.
